How to use an ACBuy spreadsheet

Use product references to narrow your options, then verify the details with the seller and your chosen purchasing service.

01 / FIND & COMPARE

Build a useful shortlist

Search by product type, then compare construction, fabric notes and available variants. Save the source item ID alongside the listing so similar products do not get mixed up.

  • Check the exact color and variant.
  • Compare garment measurements with an item you own.
  • For shoes, request relevant sizing measurements.
02 / CHECK BEFORE PAYING

Confirm the purchasing route

A catalog entry does not establish current stock or agent support. Confirm that your chosen service accepts the original marketplace link and can ship the item to your destination.

  • Read current platform notices and restrictions.
  • Ask about returns and inspection options.
  • Keep order records and seller communications.
03 / REVIEW & SHIP

Inspect the actual order

If warehouse inspection is available, review photos of your own item. Check the size label, visible condition and requested measurements before approving shipment.

  • Catalog photos are not order-specific QC.
  • Check packed dimensions as well as weight.
  • Compare route terms and total parcel charges.

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Understand the links, prices and checks behind your next shortlist.

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

An ACBuy spreadsheet organizes product references to help shoppers compare items before choosing a seller or purchasing service. This page is an independent guide with a catalog snapshot from HipobuyFind.

Do these buttons open ACBuy?

No. The ACBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons open HipobuyFind. This website is not an official ACBuy website and does not process orders.

Are the listed prices the final delivered cost?

No. Prices are catalog references in CNY. Domestic delivery, purchasing fees, international freight, payment conversion and applicable taxes may add to the total.

Are the product images QC photos?

The images shown here are catalog images from the source website. They are not presented as warehouse inspection photos or proof of authenticity.

Can every item be ordered through ACBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Check current platform availability, product restrictions and shipping eligibility directly with the purchasing service before paying.

What should I save when comparing finds?

Keep the original listing URL, item ID, selected variant, measurements, dated price and any seller notes. For a completed order, also keep inspection images, payment records and parcel tracking.
